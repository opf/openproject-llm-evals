### Steps to reproduce

*   In the Meetings module, go to &#39;My meetings&#39;, &#39;Recurring meetings&#39; or &#39;All meetings&#39;

*   Make sure the list has at least a few upcoming meetings


### What is the buggy behavior?

*   The Duration column is always expressed in hours

    *   eg. &quot;0.5 h&quot;, &quot;1 h&quot;


### What is the expected behavior?

*   The Duration column should show this information in a more human-friendly format,

    *   eg. &quot;40 seconds&quot;, &quot;30 minutes&quot;, &quot;2 hours&quot;
